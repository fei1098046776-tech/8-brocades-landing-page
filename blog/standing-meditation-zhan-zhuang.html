How to Practice Zhan Zhuang Standing Meditation

Published: May 22, 2026

Called 'standing like a tree,' Zhan Zhuang is the single most powerful exercise in the Qigong tradition.

Basic Posture

Feet shoulder-width apart. Knees slightly bent (never locked). Arms held as if embracing a large tree at chest height. Eyes soft, gaze slightly downward.

Alignment Points

  • Crown of head gently lifted
  • Shoulders relaxed and dropped
  • Chest soft, not puffed
  • Lower back long, tail slightly tucked
  • Weight distributed evenly across both feet

How Long to Practice

Start with 3-5 minutes. Add 1 minute per week. Advanced practitioners stand for 30-60 minutes. Never force duration.

What to Expect

Legs will shake — this is normal. It means the deep muscles are being engaged. Trembling indicates energy moving through blocked areas.

Mental Practice

Visualize roots growing from your feet into the earth. Feel the ground supporting you completely. Let the mind quiet without forcing stillness.
