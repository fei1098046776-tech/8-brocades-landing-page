Qigong Exercises for Better Digestion and Gut Health

Published: June 03, 2026

In Traditional Chinese Medicine, digestion is governed by the Spleen and Stomach organs. Chronic stress redirects blood flow away from the digestive tract, leading to issues like bloating and indigestion. Qigong helps restore balance.

The Power of Abdominal Breathing

Deep belly breathing acts as a gentle physical massage for your internal organs, promoting peristalsis (the movement of food through the intestines) and improving gut motility.

Key Movement: Separate Heaven and Earth

This movement involves stretching one hand up and one hand down, which gently stretches the spleen and stomach area, stimulating digestion and energy flow.

Practice Tip

Wait at least 30-45 minutes after eating a meal before practicing Qigong to allow the body's energy to focus on digestion.
