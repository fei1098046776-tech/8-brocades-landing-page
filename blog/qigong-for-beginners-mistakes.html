5 Beginner Qigong Mistakes That Slow Your Progress

Published: May 16, 2026

Most beginners plateau early — not from lack of effort, but from preventable mistakes.

1. Tensing Instead of Relaxing

Qigong works through releasing tension, not creating it. Scan your body constantly and soften any areas of tightness.

2. Rushing Through Movements

Speed kills the benefit. The slower you move in Qigong, the more internal awareness you develop.

3. Holding the Breath

Beginners often hold breath during challenging transitions. Keep breathing naturally, even if the coordination is imperfect.

4. Practicing Only When Feeling Good

Qigong is most valuable on your worst days. Even 5 minutes during fatigue or stress produces measurable benefit.

5. Skipping the Standing Meditation

Zhan Zhuang (standing pole) may feel boring, but it's where the deepest qi cultivation happens. Practice it before every session.
