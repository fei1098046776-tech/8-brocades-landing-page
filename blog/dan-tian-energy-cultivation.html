Understanding the Dan Tian: The Energy Center of Qigong

Published: June 01, 2026

In Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) and martial arts, the Lower Dan Tian is considered the center of gravity and the main reservoir of Qi (vital energy) in the human body. Understanding it is crucial to deepening your Qigong practice.

Where is the Lower Dan Tian?

It is located approximately two inches below your navel and about one and a half inches inside the body. It corresponds with the body's physical center of mass.

How to Activate It

Use abdominal breathing. Inhale and feel your lower abdomen expand, visualizing energy filling this space. Exhale and feel the abdomen contract, storing the energy in the center.

Benefits of Dan Tian Focus

Focusing on this center during practice helps ground your weight, lowers your center of gravity, improves balance, and calms an overactive mind by drawing energy down from the head.
