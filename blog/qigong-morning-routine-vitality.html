10-Minute Qigong Morning Routine for All-Day Energy

Published: June 03, 2026

Instead of reaching for caffeine first thing in the morning, try waking up your body with a 10-minute Qigong routine. This gentle sequence wakes up the joints, improves circulation, and clears morning brain fog.

Step 1: Knocking on the Gate of Life (3 Minutes)

Twist your torso side to side, letting your loose arms gently tap your lower back and lower abdomen. This wakes up the spine and stimulates the Kidneys.

Step 2: Embracing the Tree (3 Minutes)

Stand quietly in the Zhan Zhuang posture. Breathe deeply into the lower belly to ground your energy and focus your mind for the day ahead.

Step 3: Shower of Qi (4 Minutes)

Raise your arms to the sides as you inhale, gathering energy. Exhale and bring your hands down the center line of your body, imagining a wave of relaxation clearing your mind.
