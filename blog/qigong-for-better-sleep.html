Qigong for Better Sleep: Evening Routine That Works

Published: May 28, 2026

Sleep disorders affect 30% of adults. Qigong provides a natural, medication-free path to restoration.

Why Qigong Improves Sleep

Slow Qigong movements lower cortisol, reduce heart rate, and shift the nervous system from sympathetic (alert) to parasympathetic (rest) mode.

The 15-Minute Evening Routine

Minutes 1-5: Gentle Neck and Shoulder Rolls

Release the tension accumulated from sitting during the day. Move slowly and breathe deeply.

Minutes 5-10: Standing Sway

Stand with feet hip-width apart, eyes closed. Sway gently side to side like seaweed in water. Let the mind go blank.

Minutes 10-15: Lying Down Qigong Breath

Lie flat on your back. Place hands on belly. Breathe abdominally — 4 counts in, 6 counts out. Repeat 20-30 cycles.

Best Timing

Practice 1-2 hours before bed. Avoid vigorous practice within 30 minutes of sleep — it can be energizing.
