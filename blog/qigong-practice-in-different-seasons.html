How to Adapt Your Qigong Practice to the Seasons: TCM Guide

Published: June 02, 2026

Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) teaches that humans are a part of nature. To maintain optimal health, we must align our lifestyle and practice with the changing seasons. Here is how to adapt your Qigong routine.

1. Spring (Wood Element)

Focus on twisting and stretching movements to nourish the Liver and Gallbladder. Practice outdoors if possible to connect with the rising green energy.

2. Summer (Fire Element)

Keep movements slow and gentle to avoid overheating the Heart. Focus on deep, cooling exhalations and practice in the early morning before the heat peaks.

3. Autumn (Metal Element)

Focus on movements that expand the chest to strengthen the Lungs. Practice deep breathing to prepare your immune system for the cold season.

4. Winter (Water Element)

Nourish the Kidneys. Move slowly and conserve your energy. Focus on standing meditation (Zhan Zhuang) and warm, grounding postures.
