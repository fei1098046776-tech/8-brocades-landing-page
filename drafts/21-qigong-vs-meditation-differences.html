Qigong vs. Sitting Meditation: Which Is Best for Stress?

Published: [DATE]

Traditional sitting meditation is a powerful practice, but for many people with high stress or anxiety, sitting still in silence can actually trigger restless thoughts. Qigong offers a compelling alternative: moving meditation.

The Challenge of Sitting Still

When you sit still, your mind has nothing to focus on but your thoughts. For a stressed mind, this often leads to ruminating. Qigong provides a physical anchor — slow movement and breath coordination — which occupies the active mind more easily.

Mind-Body Integration

Qigong integrates movement, breath, and visualization. By focusing on the feeling of weight shifting and the physical alignment of your joints, the mind naturally settles into the present moment without forced effort.

Which Should You Choose?

If you find sitting still difficult or experience physical discomfort during meditation, start with Qigong. Once your nervous system calms down, sitting meditation will feel much easier to practice.
