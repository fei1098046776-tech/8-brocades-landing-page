Gentle Qigong Movements for Relieving Lower Back Pain

Published: [DATE]

Lower back pain is often caused by a combination of tight muscles, poor posture, and a lack of blood circulation. Unlike high-impact exercises that can aggravate spinal issues, Qigong offers a gentle, restorative solution.

Spine Mobilization in Qigong

Qigong movements focus on gentle rotation, flexion, and extension of the spine. This increases the flow of synovial fluid between the vertebrae, keeping the spinal discs lubricated and flexible.

Key Movements for Lower Back Relief

  • Two Hands Support the Heavens: Stretches the entire spine and releases compression in the vertebrae.
  • Wise Owl Gazes Backward: Rotates the neck and spine gently, releasing tension in the upper and lower back muscles.

Guidelines for Safe Practice

Keep your knees slightly bent to absorb impact and support the pelvis. Never push into sharp pain — move only within your comfortable range of motion.
