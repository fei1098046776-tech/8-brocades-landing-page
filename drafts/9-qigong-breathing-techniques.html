Qigong Breathing Techniques for Deep Calm and Energy

Published: May 14, 2026

Breath is the foundation of all Qigong practice. Before mastering movement, master your breath.

The Natural Breath

Qigong begins with natural breathing — no force, no control. Observe the breath and let it settle before adding movement.

Abdominal (Dan Tian) Breathing

Inhale and allow the belly to expand outward. Exhale and gently draw the navel inward. This activates the parasympathetic nervous system within minutes.

Reverse Breathing

Advanced technique: inhale while contracting the abdomen, exhale while expanding. Builds internal pressure and energy cultivation.

The 4-7-8 Qigong Breath

Inhale for 4 counts, hold for 7, exhale slowly for 8. Proven to reduce cortisol and induce calm within 3-4 cycles.

Integration With Movement

In 8-Brocade practice, each movement is paired with either an inhale or exhale. Inhale on opening movements, exhale on closing or pressing movements.
