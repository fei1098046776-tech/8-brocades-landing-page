The 800-Year History of 8-Brocade Qigong

Published: June 1, 2026

The 8-Brocades (Ba Duan Jin) is one of China's oldest and most practiced health cultivation systems.

Origins: Song Dynasty (960-1279 CE)

First recorded references appear in the Song Dynasty military manual 'Yi Jian Zhi.' Designed to maintain soldier fitness between campaigns.

Attribution to Marshal Yue Fei

Popular tradition credits Song Dynasty General Yue Fei with creating the standing 8-Brocades to build troop health and vitality. Historical evidence is debated but the attribution persists culturally.

Ming and Qing Dynasty Development

The form was refined and standardized during the Ming Dynasty. Taoist and Buddhist monasteries adopted it as part of their daily health cultivation routines.

Modern Standardization

In 1957, the Chinese government commissioned the first standardized version for national health promotion. A revised standard was released in 2003 and remains the most widely taught form today.

Global Spread

Today, an estimated 100 million people practice some form of 8-Brocade Qigong globally — making it one of the most widely practiced wellness systems in human history.
