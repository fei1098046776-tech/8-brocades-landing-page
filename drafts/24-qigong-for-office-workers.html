5-Minute Qigong Desk Routine for Office Workers

Published: [DATE]

Sitting at a desk for hours causes rounded shoulders, tight hips, and neck stiffness. This simple, 5-minute Qigong routine can be done right next to your desk to restore circulation and release tension.

1. Shaking the Body (1 Minute)

Stand up, bounce on your heels, and let your arms hang loose. Shake your entire body to release physical tension and reset your posture.

2. Wise Owl Gazes Backward (2 Minutes)

Stand straight. Inhale, slowly rotate your hands outward, and turn your head to look over your shoulder. Exhale, return to center. Releases tightness in the neck and upper back.

3. Pulling Heaven and Earth (2 Minutes)

Inhale, raise your arms. Exhale, press one hand up to the ceiling and the other down to the floor. Creates space in the spine and opens up the ribcage.

Summary

Repeat this routine every 2-3 hours to boost energy and prevent repetitive strain injuries.
