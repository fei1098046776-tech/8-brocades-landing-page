What to Eat to Support Your Qigong Practice: TCM Food Guide

Published: May 26, 2026

In TCM, food is medicine. What you eat directly affects your Qi (energy) and the depth of your practice.

Foods That Build Qi

  • Whole grains: brown rice, oats, millet
  • Root vegetables: sweet potato, yam, carrot
  • Lean protein: chicken, fish, eggs, legumes
  • Dates (Jujube): traditional Qi tonic

Foods That Deplete Qi

  • Cold raw foods (reduce digestive fire)
  • Excess sugar and processed foods
  • Alcohol (disperses Qi)
  • Excessive caffeine

Eating Timing for Practitioners

Practice Qigong on an empty or lightly filled stomach. Wait 30 minutes after practice before eating a full meal to allow Qi to settle.

The Spleen-Stomach System

TCM considers the Spleen and Stomach as the source of Qi production. Warm, cooked foods strengthen this system far more than cold salads and smoothies.
